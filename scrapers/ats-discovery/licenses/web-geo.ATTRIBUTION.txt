ATS Discovery's generated geography snapshot is derived from the Web Core
artifact at backend/core/static/data/geo.sample8.json.

Countries: mledoze/countries (ODbL 1.0)
https://github.com/mledoze/countries

Cities: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0)
https://www.geonames.org/

The generated snapshot is refreshed by tools/refresh_ats_geo_snapshot.py and
scrapers/ats-discovery/scripts/refresh-web-geo-snapshot.mjs.
